# VidForge Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026. Publisher: Sunny Crafts. Contact: developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com

VidForge works on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any information about you. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics.

**What the app reads.** Only the folders you choose with Android's folder picker. To list your videos it reads file names, sizes, dates and video details (length, size, format), and keeps them in a private database on your device.

**What the app creates.** New video copies in Movies/VidForge or a folder you choose. It does not change or delete your original videos. Location (GPS) data is removed from new copies unless you choose to keep it; other details inside a video file may remain.

**History and problem log.** Kept on your device. The problem log records events and error types, without file names or folder paths. A history export includes video and folder names. The log or an export leaves your device only if you choose to share or save it somewhere yourself; the service you pick then handles it under its own policy.

**Deleting data.** Removing a folder in the app removes its videos from the list. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes its database, settings and log. Videos you created stay on your device until you delete them. Android backup is turned off for this app.

**Permissions.** Notifications, a foreground service and keeping the processor awake, so that videos you queued can finish while you use other apps or the screen is off.

**Changes.** Updates to this policy are posted on this page.
