# Keywise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026. Applies to Keywise version 0.1.0 and later until replaced.

## The short version

Keywise is a free English keyboard from Sunny Crafts. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It collects nothing off your device. Words and word pairs it learns stay in the app's private storage on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

When you type and commit words, Keywise learns those words and short word pairs to improve suggestions, swipe typing and automatic correction. A new word is kept only as a candidate until you use it several times or choose to keep it. Unused candidates are deleted after 30 days. Keywise does not store whole messages. Nothing is sent to Sunny Crafts or any other service. Incognito mode pauses learning. Learning and suggestions are off in password fields and in editors that ask for no personalized learning. Android cloud backup is off for Keywise.

## Files you create

If you choose to back up your Keywise data, the app creates a file with your personal vocabulary and related settings and saves it where you choose with Android's file picker. You control that file. Restoring a file imports it into Keywise. Delete backup copies you no longer want.

## Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android handle information under their own policies. Android shows a system warning when you enable any third-party keyboard. Keywise has no internet permission and does not transmit what you type.

## No selling or sharing

Sunny Crafts does not sell or share your typed text or personal vocabulary. Keywise does not transmit them.

## Deleting your data

Use Delete all Keywise data in the app to remove its local data and settings. You can also remove single words or clear your personal dictionary. Uninstalling Keywise removes its private data. Delete saved backup files yourself.

## Children

Keywise is a general-purpose keyboard and is not designed for children. It does not knowingly collect children's information, because it collects no personal information.

## Changes

If the app's behaviour or the law changes, this policy may be updated. The latest version shows its effective date.
