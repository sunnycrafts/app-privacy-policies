# FuelTracker: Privacy Policy

**App:** FuelTracker (Android, package `com.sunnycrafts.fueltracker`)
**Developer:** Sunny Crafts
**Contact:** developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 17 November 2026 (first release)
**Applies to:** version 1.0.0 and later, until this policy is changed

## 1. The short version
FuelTracker keeps your fuel and vehicle records on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app asks for no permissions, including no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. Something leaves your phone only when you choose to save or share it.

## 2. What stays on your phone
- Your vehicles, fill-ups, expenses, service records and saved stations, and your settings (theme, colours, larger text, graphics on or off).
- The picture of a vehicle, if you choose one. Android's photo picker gives the app only that one picture. A reduced copy is kept in the app's private storage.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so nothing is copied to Google Drive or another phone automatically.

## 3. Files you create
Export CSV and Back up create a file only when you tap them, in the place you pick with Android's own save screen. Import CSV and Restore read only the single file you pick. These files hold your own records, so keep them somewhere private. Restore and Import only add records that are missing. They do not overwrite or delete what is already in the app.

## 4. Other apps you open
- Find a picture online opens your own browser with the vehicle text you typed. The app does not load the page and does not see the result. What the browser or search site does is governed by its own policy.
- Open in Maps passes a station address or coordinates you saved to your maps app, only when you tap it.

## 5. Google Play and Android
If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation and, if you allowed it in your phone settings, crash and performance reports (Android vitals), under Google's own privacy policy. The app adds no crash-reporting, analytics or advertising library and has no advertising ID.

## 6. No selling or sharing
Your information is not collected by the developer, and it is not sold, rented, shared or used for advertising or profiling.

## 7. Deleting your data
Delete a record inside the app, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. The developer holds no copy of your data.

## 8. Children
The app is a general-purpose tool and is not aimed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

## 9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date.
