# Secure Gallery Privacy Policy

Date: 17 November 2026. App version 1.0.0. Developer: Sunny Crafts. Contact: developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com

## Short version
Secure Gallery does not collect, send or sell your data. Your photos, videos, tags, ratings, settings and vault stay on your phone.

## What the app reads
- Photos and videos on your phone, through the media permission you grant (all, or only the ones you pick). They are shown on the phone and are not uploaded.
- The fingerprint or face check, if you turn it on. Android does this; the app only learns yes or no.

## What the app stores on your phone
- A list of your media for fast search, tags, ratings, favorites and settings, in the app's private storage.
- The vault: files encrypted with keys made from your PIN or passphrase and your Recovery Key. The developer cannot open it. If you lose both, nobody can.
- A local log without photo names or vault names. You can read it in the app.
- A trial marker (first start and latest time seen) kept by Google Play services Block Store on this phone only.

## What leaves the phone
Nothing from the app's own code. The app makes no network calls of its own. Google Play handles the one-time purchase, and Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that. Report A Problem and Send A Suggestion open an e-mail draft that you read, edit and send yourself. The app sends nothing by itself.

## What we do not do
No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no advertising ID, no automatic crash reports, no sharing with third parties.

## Children
The app is not aimed at children under 13.

## Your choices
Delete the app to remove its data. Export or erase the vault in the app. Revoke the media permission in Android settings at any time.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above changes and the What's New page in the app says so.
