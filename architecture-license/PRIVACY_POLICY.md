# Architecture License: Privacy Policy

App: Architecture License (Android), package com.sunny.archlicense. Developer: sunnycrafts. Contact: developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com. Effective date: 9 November 2026. Applies to version 1.0.0 and later until this policy is changed.

**1. The short version.** Your study progress, notes, logs, name and picture stay on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Its own code makes no network calls and sends nothing. Something leaves your phone only when you choose to share, save or e-mail it.

**2. What stays on your phone.** Weeks and checklists, board questions and answers, flashcards, practice results, the four logs (mistakes, code references, experience, unresolved questions), study time, favorites and lists, self-assessment, settings, your optional name and picture, the trial dates and a small diagnostic log. They are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so nothing is copied to Google Drive automatically.

**3. What the app does not use.** The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar, SMS or call logs, and does not ask for those permissions. It does not read your other files or photos.

**4. Your picture.** If you choose a picture, Android's photo picker gives the app only that one picture. A small 320-pixel copy is kept inside the app. It is never part of exports or problem reports. You can remove it at any time (Me, Personalize, Remove My Picture).

**5. Reading aloud.** The speaker buttons use your phone's own text-to-speech engine (a separate app you choose in Android settings). The app passes the text to that engine on your phone; it does not send it anywhere itself. The engine works under its own settings and policy.

**6. Trial and purchase.** The first 30 days are a free trial. After that, a one-time purchase through Google Play unlocks the study screens for good; there is no subscription. Google Play handles the payment and the purchase record under Google's own privacy policy. The app stores only "purchased: yes or no" and the trial dates. For the purchase, the Google Play Billing library adds the INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and BILLING permissions; the app's own code does not use them. Your records, settings, exports and this policy stay open even if you do not buy.

**7. Problem reports and suggestions.** Send Feedback opens your own e-mail app with a message you can edit. If you leave "Attach the diagnostic log" on, a text file is attached with the app version, your phone model and Android version, and the app's diagnostic log. The log lists actions, counts and error types only; it never contains your progress, answers, notes, log entries, name or picture. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your e-mail app, and the developer then receives only what you sent, including your e-mail address. Messages are used only to answer you and to fix problems, and are not shared.

**8. Google's own reporting.** If Android's crash and usage reporting (Android vitals, run by Google) is turned on on your phone, Google may collect crash and freeze reports under its own policy and show anonymous summaries to the developer. Google Play also gives the developer anonymous install and rating statistics. The app adds no crash-reporting, analytics or advertising library, and has no advertising ID.

**9. Exports.** "Save Data File", "Share Data File", "Share Text Summary" and "Save All Logs as Markdown" create files only when you tap them, through Android's own save and share screens. You decide where they go.

**10. Links.** Official links (licensing boards and other sources) open in your own browser. The app itself does not load web pages.

**11. No selling or sharing.** Your information is not sold, rented, shared or used for advertising.

**12. Children.** The app is a study tool for adults planning a professional license. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

**13. Your control and deleting your data.** You can export your data, import it on another phone (or the Windows app), reset all progress (Me, Your Data, Reset All Progress, with "Share a copy first"), or uninstall the app. Uninstalling, or clearing the app's storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored on the phone; files you saved elsewhere stay. To have an e-mail you sent deleted, write to developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com.

**14. Security.** Files are written safely so a crash cannot leave a half-written file; a damaged file is set aside and the last good copy is used. Exports are plain files: keep them somewhere private if they contain personal notes.

**15. Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update, is shown here and on the policy's web page, with a new effective date. Questions: developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com
