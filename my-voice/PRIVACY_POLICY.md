# My Voice: AI Writing Help (Wordprint): Privacy Policy

**App:** My Voice: AI Writing Help, also called Wordprint (Android, package `com.sunnycrafts.wordprint`)
**Developer:** Sunny Crafts
**Contact:** developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 2026-11-24
**Applies to:** version 2.0.0 and later, until this policy is changed

## 1. The short version

Wordprint builds a picture of your own writing style on your phone. Your writing, your word list, your style profile, your answers, your voice memos and your settings stay on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Its own code makes no network calls and sends nothing. Something leaves your phone only when you choose to share, copy, save or e-mail it.

## 2. What stays on your phone

- The text you paste and the files you pick, the word list and style profile built from them.
- The answers you write or speak in Write and Practice, your voice memo recordings and their transcripts, the words you add, and your favorites and lists.
- Your settings, including the name, picture or emoji you choose in Personalize, and the choices you make for your Voice Profile.
- The trial and purchase state: whether you bought the app, and the latest date the app has seen (used so that moving the phone's clock back cannot extend the trial). The start of the trial comes from Android's own record of when the app was installed.
- A small diagnostic log of what the app did (for example "saved a practice answer" or an error type). The log never contains your writing, answers, memos, words, name or picture. It is limited in size and old entries are removed.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so nothing is copied to Google Drive or another phone automatically.

## 3. Files and text you give the app

The app reads only the text you paste and the files you pick yourself with Android's file picker (.txt, .md and .docx). It does not look at your other files, photos or folders. You tick which pieces are your own writing, and only those shape your profile. You can remove any piece at any time.

## 4. Your Voice Profile and sharing it with an AI

The Give This to an AI screen creates text containing the instructions, style notes and word list you chose to include (and a few of your own sentences, only if you switch that on). You see it before it goes anywhere. It leaves the phone only when you tap Copy to Clipboard, Share as a Markdown File or Share as Text. Copying puts the text on Android's clipboard, which other apps on your phone may be able to read until it is replaced. Sharing opens Android's share sheet and the text goes to the app you pick (for example an AI chat app). Wordprint does not send it anywhere itself and is not connected to any AI service. What another app or service does with the profile you give it is governed by that app's or service's own terms and privacy policy.

## 5. Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only while you press Record (Voice Memos) or Speak (Speak Your Answer). Nothing is recorded in the background. A Speak Your Answer recording is deleted as soon as it has been turned into text. Memo recordings stay on your phone until you delete them. Speech is turned into text by Android's on-device speech recognizer, which the app asks to work on the phone; if your phone needs its offline English speech pack, your phone's own speech service downloads it when you press Download, not this app. Read Aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine and only offline voices. Those services work under their own settings and policies.

## 6. Your picture

If you choose a picture, Android's photo picker gives the app only that one picture. A small 320-pixel copy is kept inside the app and is never part of reports. You can remove it at any time.

## 7. Trial and purchase

The first 30 days are a free trial. After that, a one-time purchase through Google Play unlocks the app for good; there is no subscription. Google Play handles the payment and the purchase record under Google's own privacy policy. The app stores only whether you purchased. For the purchase, the Google Play Billing library adds the INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and BILLING permissions to the app; the app's own code does not use them to send your writing or your data. The Billing library itself talks to Google Play for the purchase and may send Google technical information about that connection, under Google's own privacy policy. After the trial ends, Check, Write, Practice, Memos and the Profile pages need the purchase; your own data, exports, settings and this policy stay open.

## 8. Problem reports and suggestions

Send Feedback opens your own e-mail app with a message you can edit and addressed to developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com. If you leave "Attach the diagnostic log" on, a text file is attached with the app version, your phone model, Android version and the app's diagnostic log. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your e-mail app. If you send it, sunnycrafts receives what you sent, including your e-mail address, and uses it only to answer you and fix problems. Messages are not shared.

## 9. Other things you can export

Share My Progress (a data file or a text summary), the back-to-PC file and Save Log are created only when you tap them, through Android's own share and save screens. The data file contains your answers, memo transcripts, added words, favorites, lists, settings and the pieces of writing you added, which is your own text, so keep it somewhere private. Memo recordings are not included. You decide where each file goes.

## 10. Google Play and Android

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation and, if you allowed it in your phone's settings, crash and performance reports (Android vitals), under Google's own privacy policy. sunnycrafts may see these only as anonymous totals in the Play Console. The app adds no crash-reporting, analytics or advertising library and has no advertising ID.

## 11. No selling or sharing

Your information is not sold, rented, shared or used for advertising or profiling.

## 12. Children

Wordprint is a writing tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

## 13. Your control and deleting your data

You can delete any answer, memo, piece of writing or added word, erase everything at once with **Me > Settings > Erase Phone Data** (it offers to share a copy first), or uninstall the app. Uninstalling, or clearing the app's storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored on the phone. Files, text and profiles you copied, saved or shared elsewhere stay where you put them. To have an e-mail you sent deleted, write to developer.sunnycrafts@gmail.com.

## 14. Security

Files are written safely so that a crash cannot leave a half-written file; a damaged file is set aside and the last good copy is used. Exports and profiles are plain files, so keep them somewhere private: they contain your own words.

## 15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update, is shown in the app (**Me > Privacy Policy**) and on its web page, with a new effective date.
